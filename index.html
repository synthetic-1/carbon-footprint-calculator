THE BIG PICTURE

What Is a Carbon Footprint?

A carbon footprint is the amount of greenhouse gases released into the atmosphere by a person, activity, or product. These gases trap heat and contribute to climate change.

Transportation, electricity, food, and the things we buy all affect our footprint. Understanding these effects helps us make more environmentally responsible choices.

MAKE A DIFFERENCE

Small Steps, Lighter Impact.

Start with one practical change and build better habits over time.

01. Travel

Walk, cycle, carpool, or use public transport when practical and safe. Combining trips can reduce unnecessary driving.

02. Energy

Switch off unused lights, choose efficient appliances when possible, and use electricity thoughtfully.

03. Food

Plan meals, store food correctly, and use leftovers to reduce food waste.
